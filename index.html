FAQ

Questions, answered

How do you decide what size system I need?

We start with your electricity bills. By looking at how much power you use and when, we size a system that cuts your bill as much as possible without paying for capacity you will not use.

How much can I save?

Many customers can reduce their electricity bill by up to 80%. Your exact savings depend on your usage, roof and system size, and we show you the numbers before you commit.

Will solar work in Kashmir's winters?

Yes. We simulate every system against local conditions before installing, and panel-level monitoring and optimisation help keep output up even when one panel is affected by shade or snow.

Can I keep power during outages?

Yes, with a backup or battery storage system. We design it to keep your essential loads running when the grid is down.

What happens if a component fails?

If a component fails during the warranty period, we aim to replace it within 24–48 hours to keep downtime to a minimum.

Can I see how my system is performing?

Yes. Our monitoring shows your generation, consumption, grid import and export, and savings in real time.
